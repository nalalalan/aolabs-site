crème brûlée
  • Use the Serious Eats method: equal parts cream and whole milk, vanilla, egg yolks, sugar, and salt.
  • Temper hot dairy into yolks slowly; strain so the custard is completely smooth.
  • Bake low in a water bath until the centers wobble; chill at least 4 hours.
  • Torch a thin, even sugar layer right before eating so the top cracks instead of sweating.
