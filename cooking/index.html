S
avocado
  • Start with a clean avocado pit or a young avocado tree.
  • For a pit, suspend the broad end in water until roots and a shoot form, then pot it in fast-draining soil. For a young tree, plant it directly in fast-draining soil.
  • Grow in full sun with wind and frost protection. Water deeply, keep coarse mulch off the trunk, and let the soil drain between waterings.
  • Harvest a mature avocado, then ripen it at room temperature until it yields to gentle palm pressure.
  • Wash it, cut lengthwise around the pit, twist the halves apart, and scoop the pit out with a spoon.
  • Peel or spoon out the flesh. Add 1-2 g salt if needed. Eat the avocado.
  • Sources: California Avocado grow guide and cutting guide.

crème brûlée
  • Use the Serious Eats method: equal parts cream and whole milk, vanilla, egg yolks, sugar, and salt.
  • Default batch: four 7 oz ramekins filled with 6 oz custard each, 24 oz total.
  • Temper hot dairy into yolks slowly; strain so the custard is completely smooth.
  • Bake low in a water bath until the centers wobble; chill at least 4 hours.
  • Torch a thin, even sugar layer right before eating so the top cracks instead of sweating.

Point Reyes blue cheese block
  • Use Curd Nerd's blue cheese recipe.
  • Warm raw whole cow milk; stir in mesophilic starter and Penicillium roqueforti; rest so the culture acidifies the milk.
  • Add diluted microbial rennet, wait for a clean set, cut the curd, drain whey, salt, and pack loosely into a small mold.
  • Flip as it drains, then pierce the block after it firms so oxygen reaches the center and the blue mold can grow.
  • Age cold and humid until the paste turns creamy and blue-green veins spread through it.
